How the burn works

$GEMSEARCH launched on pump.fun with 1,000,000,000 tokens. A burn destroys tokens on chain, so the supply shrinks. The number at the top is simply the original 1B minus the supply Solana reports right now, so it counts every burn ever made, by anyone.

The team burns on a schedule, every 12 hours at 00:00 and 12:00 UTC, by hand from this same button. Each burn is a normal Solana transaction: click any row to see it on Solscan.

Anyone holding $GEMSEARCH can burn their own tokens here too. It cannot be undone.

Burning reduces supply. It is not a promise of price.
