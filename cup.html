Rules

  • Who: anyone who launches a coin through gemsearch.fun/launch, on Solana (pump.fun) or Robinhood Chain (pons). No need to hold $GEMSEARCH.
  • Week: Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. The coin must be launched inside the week.
  • Ranking: market cap on DexScreener when the week ends. The live table updates every minute.
  • One per creator: only your best coin counts, so launching many coins does not help.
  • Clean launches only: a Solana coin with 3 or more other buys in its launch block (a bundle) is out. Checked on chain.
  • Prizes: $50, $20 and $10, paid by the Gem Search team to the creator wallet within 48 hours after the week ends, with the transaction posted on @gemsearchfun.
  • We may exclude coins that break the law, impersonate a real project or person, or game the ranking (wash trading). Not financial advice: a prize is for the creator, not a reason to buy any coin.
