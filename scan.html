Wallets are linked when one funded the other, when one wallet funded several of them, when they passed this coin between each other, or when they bought in the same block as the launch. Exchange hot wallets never count as a shared funder. Every link opens its transaction.

The score adds up red flags that can be read on chain right now. A high score means the launch is clean, not that the coin will go up. Anything the scanner could not read is shown as unknown and never counts as a pass. Not financial advice.
