How it is scored

Every time the council convenes on gemsearch.fun, in the bot, or on its own when a tracked account drops a contract, the verdict is stored with the coin's market cap at that moment (one per coin every 6 hours). 24 hours later the spider reads the market cap again from DexScreener.

AVOID was right if the coin fell 30% or more. APE was right if it rose 30% or more. WATCH was right if it moved less than that. Coins DexScreener no longer lists count as worth zero. Nothing is hidden or hand-picked. Past calls do not predict future ones; not financial advice.
