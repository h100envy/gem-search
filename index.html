// meet the spider

The curious side of your feed.

Gem Search puts an eight-legged research companion on X. It walks between visible posts, highlights what it is inspecting and hands it to a research engine on your own computer.

🕷️

A companion with character

The spider pauses on a post and shows what it is investigating. You see the work happening, right in the tab.

🔎

Every lead has a trail

Posts, public links, checks and unknowns stay attached to the finding. Open the source. Challenge the conclusion.

🏡

Your computer is home

The engine and database run locally. Bring your own Grok key when you want model reviews. No hosted account.

// the pipeline

From a post to a decision.

Every stage is visible and every claim keeps its evidence.

01 · OBSERVE

Feed

The spider reads posts already rendered in your visible tab. No paid X API.

02 · CONNECT

JEV

Our local detector groups repeated topics, tickers and project links into narratives.

03 · INVESTIGATE

Crawler

Reads public sites, repos and docs behind a lead. Private networks are blocked.

04 · CHALLENGE

4× Grok

Optional: four reviewers argue the evidence from different angles, with references.

05 · DECIDE

You

A dashboard keeps sources, gaps and verdicts together. The call stays yours.

// local first

Runs on your laptop. Stays there.

  • Open source under MIT. Read every line.
  • Engine and database on your own machine.
  • Works without any API key using local rules.
  • Offline queue survives restarts.
// what the spider never does
  • Like, reply or follow on your behalf.
  • Touch or connect a wallet.
  • Leave the page or watch other apps.
  • Send your posts anywhere unless you turn Grok on.
// quick start

Release the spider in two minutes.

Python 3.11+ and Chrome. macOS, Linux or Windows through WSL2.

  1. Open 127.0.0.1:8787, copy the pairing code in Connections.
  2. In chrome://extensions load the extension/ folder unpacked.
  3. Paste the code in the popup, open X, click Release spider.

No X account at hand? Open /spider-demo and meet the spider on fictional posts.

# clone and run the local engine
$ git clone https://github.com/h100envy/gem-search.git
$ cd gem-search
$ cp .env.example .env
$ python3 app.py
